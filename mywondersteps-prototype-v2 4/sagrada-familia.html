One step in Barcelona

Looking up at the Sagrada Família

We did not make it inside, but the view from the street still became part of the journey.

Barcelona, SpainSummer 2026Sagrada Família

The Sagrada Família is Gaudí’s most famous work and one of the places most closely associated with Barcelona. More than a century after construction began, it is still being built, with cranes rising beside its towers and new stone standing next to older, darker façades.

We had hoped to go inside, but the tickets had sold out several days earlier. So we stayed outside and walked around the basilica, looking up from different corners of the surrounding streets.

What stayed with me

Always unfinished

From the sidewalk, the building felt less like one fixed monument and more like several generations of work growing together under the Barcelona sky.

Missing the interior was disappointing, but it also made the visit feel honest. This step was not the complete Sagrada Família experience. It was simply the view we had that day: crowded streets, a clear blue sky, cranes overhead, and a building that still had not reached its final form.

Sometimes a journey keeps the part you missed alongside the part you saw.
